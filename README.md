# Restaurant Digital Marketing Analysis

A comparative study of the digital marketing strategies used by five well-known Pakistani restaurant brands: **Butt Karahi, Cheezious, Al-Khan, Arcadian, and Cafe Zouk**.

## Overview

This project analyzes how each restaurant brand builds its identity, engages customers, and promotes itself online. It looks at social media presence, content style, pricing strategy, target audience, menu presentation, and offers practical recommendations for improving digital engagement.

## Brands Covered

| Brand | Type | Positioning |
|---|---|---|
| **Butt Karahi** | Desi / Traditional | Authentic Lahori karahi & tawa dishes, heritage-driven |
| **Cheezious** | Fast Food | "The Ultimate Cheesy Comfort" — value-focused, youth appeal |
| **Al-Khan** | Multi-Cuisine / Heritage Dining | Family-friendly fine dining with hi-tea & buffet |
| **Arcadian** | Premium Casual / Fine Dining | Sophisticated continental & Asian-fusion dining |
| **Cafe Zouk** | Specialty Coffee / Cafe | Cozy cafe culture and lifestyle branding |

## Key Findings

- **Food photography** is the single strongest driver of engagement across all five brands.
- **Value-based brands** (Cheezious, Al-Khan) compete on deals and pricing; **premium brands** (Arcadian, Cafe Zouk) avoid discounting to protect brand image.
- **Butt Karahi** relies on cultural authenticity rather than promotions.
- None of the five brands strongly market health-conscious or dietary-specific menu options — a shared gap across the industry.
- All brands would benefit from more consistent posting, deeper customer engagement, and wider influencer partnerships.

## Methodology

1. Reviewed each restaurant's official website, Instagram, Facebook, and delivery-app presence.
2. Compared content style, posting frequency, and engagement patterns.
3. Analyzed pricing and promotional strategies against each brand's market position.
4. Mapped each brand's target audience and messaging style.
5. Summarized key strengths, gaps, and actionable recommendations per brand.

## Recommendations Summary

- **Butt Karahi:** Launch TikTok, post more consistently, use local hashtags, partner with food influencers.
- **Cheezious:** Add polls/challenges, share behind-the-scenes content, reply faster to customer feedback.
- **Al-Khan:** Broaden discounts beyond desserts, speed up delivery, expand to new platforms (TikTok, X).
- **Arcadian:** Diversify beyond Instagram/Facebook, run user-generated campaigns, improve online ordering.
- **Cafe Zouk:** Increase short-form video content, start a loyalty program, collaborate with micro-influencers.
